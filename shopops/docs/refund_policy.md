# Kettle & Crate refund policy

- **Window:** items can be returned within **30 days of delivery** for a full refund to the original payment method.
- **Damaged or wrong items:** we cover return shipping and send a replacement or refund, customer's choice. Ask for a photo.
- **Opened consumables** (paper filters, beeswax wraps) are not refundable unless faulty.
- **Knives** can only be returned unused and in original packaging.
- **Cancelled before shipping:** orders in `pending` or `paid` can be cancelled for a full refund.
- **After shipping:** the order must be delivered first; then it follows the 30-day return process and is marked `refunded` once the item is back.
- **Refund timing:** refunds are issued within 5 business days of receiving the return.
- **Duplicate charges:** escalate to `urgent` and refund the duplicate the same day.
- **Goodwill:** support agents may offer a store credit of up to $25 for late deliveries (more than 5 days late) without manager approval.
